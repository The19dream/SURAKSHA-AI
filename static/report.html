Critical Containment Sequence

Step-by-step
  • 0 - 1 Hour

    Emergency Financial Freeze

    Call 1930 immediately if you transferred money via UPI, netbanking, or card. Ask the bank to place an instant hold.

    Dial National Helpline 1930
  • Within 2 Hours

    Bank Access Lockdown

    Contact your bank directly and request a lock on online banking, UPI registration, and active card transactions.

    Keep transaction reference IDs and time stamps handy.
  • Within 12 Hours

    Document Evidence

    Collect screenshots, transcripts, bank statements, suspect numbers, and profile links before chats disappear.

    Chat backup Phone numbers Debit receipts
  • Within 24 Hours

    File Official Cyber Complaint

    Open the official portal and submit your screenshots and notes to register the formal cyber grievance.

    Open cybercrime.gov.in

What to Collect

•Phone records: Screenshots of logs displaying the suspect's country code.
•Audio recordings: Call recorder logs of coercive phone or video chats.
•PDF documents: Fake court notices, CBI arrest threats, or TRAI warnings.
